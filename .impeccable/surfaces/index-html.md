# Timer Horizon surface brief

## Scope and mode
Full-viewport landing experience. Mode: **Experience**.

## Visitor
A curious visitor opens the page and immediately feels their elapsed time become physically connected to enormous, continuously happening processes.

## Task and interaction
Default the timer to the visit. The central human-language duration opens the native start-time dialog. Pointer motion creates a visual-only glyph wake; empty field clicks invite a new fact. Active facts eventually link to citations. While the start-time dialog is open, visual field time dilates; where available, a same-document View Transition carries the timer into and out of the dialog, with native-dialog and reduced-motion fallbacks.

## Content and constraints
Show four to eight live, rate-derived facts at once. Facts enter beyond the viewport and disappear at the rim; the central core stays black except for the timer. Each fact has a stable materializing number with its unit directly beside it, then a smaller explainer beneath (wrapping to two lines only when needed); individual facts update on staggered three-to-five-second rhythms. Every visible fact steadily bends and brightens nearby abstract glyphs through a bounded, fact-local lens; it has no arrival pulse or conjunction animation. Facts must be readable HTML links; the background glyph field is abstract but visibly made of letters and numbers. Reduced motion keeps the full interface usable without the animated sequences. V1's fact catalogue reserves but does not verify sources.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment
**The Observatory Edition.** A broad, all-direction glyph infall fills the perimeter with slow blue, violet, and green spectral gradients while a brighter upper stratum folds into a compact luminous event horizon. Thin ember, orange, mineral, and spectral accretion bands orbit a black core held inside their rim; the composed central duration is the sole human anchor. An empty-field click sends a prismatic current through the glyphs but does not add content. Each readable fact holds a larger, steady local glyph lens and color burst while its number materializes in place before the whole body disappears at the rim; its departure sends a brief current inward.

## Unresolved decisions
Verified source catalogue and long-duration precision policy are follow-up work.
