# Timer Horizon surface brief

## Scope and mode
Full-viewport landing experience. Mode: **Experience**.

## Visitor
A curious visitor opens the page and immediately feels their elapsed time become physically connected to enormous, continuously happening processes.

## Task and interaction
Default the timer to the visit. The central human-language duration opens a custom start-time modal. Pointer motion creates a visual-only glyph wake; empty field clicks invite a new fact. Active facts eventually link to citations.

## Content and constraints
Show four to eight live, rate-derived facts at once. Facts enter beyond the viewport and disappear at the rim; the central core stays black except for the timer. Each fact has a stable materializing number with its unit directly beside it, then a smaller explainer beneath (wrapping to two lines only when needed); individual facts update on staggered three-to-five-second rhythms. Nearby glyphs orbit it before they fall inward. Facts must be readable HTML links; the background glyph field is abstract but visibly made of letters and numbers. Prefer a reduced-motion fallback. V1's fact catalogue reserves but does not verify sources.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment
**The Observatory Edition.** A broad, all-direction glyph infall fills the perimeter while a brighter upper stratum folds into an uneven luminous event horizon. The rim breathes at rest while a bright seam precesses around it; the composed central duration is the sole human anchor. The memorable moment is a fact number materializing in place as a small glyph orbit catches around it, before the whole body disappears at the rim.

## Unresolved decisions
Verified source catalogue and long-duration precision policy are follow-up work.
