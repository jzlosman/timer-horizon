## Design Specificity Verdict

The visual language is specific to Timer Horizon: the warm broken ring, human-language clock, and refusal of dashboard chrome are authored. The composition is not yet equally specific. The central group is geometrically centered, but the implementation has no vertical-density plan, upper fact role, or optical center. Both independent assessments confirmed that the upper region reads as vacant black rather than charged space.

## Deterministic and browser evidence

The detector returned `[]` for `index.html` (0 findings). The 1440×900 browser capture succeeded. It confirmed sparse top space, a middle-third concentration around the ring, and a small low centered instruction. No injected overlay was created.

## Overall Impression

The current page is a strong instrument trapped in a weak stage. The ring is memorable; the surrounding field has not yet taken responsibility for the entire viewport.

## What's Working

- The central duration is calm, legible, and owns the first read.
- The palette is restrained: mineral text and a single amber/ember source of heat.
- The DOM content layer and reduced-motion fallback preserve the core experience.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] The top third is dead canvas
**Why it matters:** It makes the experience feel partially loaded and pushes all visual mass below the optical center.
**Fix:** Shift the horizon/timer group upward to about 44–46vh. Seed sparse, low-heat facts in the upper perimeter and send a cool mineral current across that region before it bends into the ring.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout` then `/impeccable animate`.

### [P1] The field is an accretion band, not a world-scale current
**Why it matters:** The shader concentrates glyphs and heat near the center/lower band, so the full viewport does not feel inhabited.
**Fix:** Increase vertical envelope and introduce a quiet upper layer with different speed/depth. Keep it abstract; do not add a second motif or readable pseudo-data.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable animate`.

### [P2] Fact positions are incidental rather than composed
**Why it matters:** The timer carries the composition alone and repeated screenshots can put too many facts near the horizon edge.
**Fix:** Reserve two explicit upper exterior lanes and two lower exterior lanes, with stable vertical spacing. Facts must never compete with the timer’s protected void.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`.

### [P2] The bottom instruction lowers the center of gravity without earning it
**Why it matters:** Its dim permanence makes the reading path end at the viewport floor rather than cycling through the field.
**Fix:** Make it a transient first-visit trace, then remove it after the visitor has had a chance to act.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`.

### [P3] Timer control is not discoverable enough
**Why it matters:** First-time visitors may never discover custom start time.
**Fix:** Give the caption an on-hover/focus state that briefly changes to `choose a beginning` without adding chrome.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable delight`.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Passive wonder-seeker:** may read the open top as missing content before enough facts arrive to establish the system.
- **Low-vision visitor:** wordmark and instruction are too tiny/dim to provide useful orientation or interaction guidance.
- **Evidence-conscious visitor:** provisional facts look highly authoritative while source fields are deliberately empty; this remains a content-risk follow-up, not a visual solution.

## Minor Observations

- The top wordmark is currently the only upper landmark, but it is too weak to balance the lower heat.
- The ring may remain the one saturated object; do not solve the emptiness by adding color everywhere.
- The screenshot evaluator could not capture an iPhone device due the installed CLI/channel mismatch; source review and desktop evidence guided this critique.
